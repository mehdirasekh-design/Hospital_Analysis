
Hospital Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Hospital Management and Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.
The main objective is to analyze hospital performance, patient activity, admission trends, doctor performance, financial metrics, and service quality through interactive visualizations and data-driven insights.
The dashboard helps management monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify operational trends, and support informed decision-making.
Business Objectives
•	Monitor hospital appointments and patient 

activity.
•	Analyze patient demographics and behavior.
•	Evaluate hospital admission and discharge trends.
•	Measure doctor and department performance.
•	Analyze hospital billing and revenue.
•	Monitor service quality and operational efficiency.
Tools & Technologies

Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of hospital performance, including total patients, appointments, revenue, and appointment completion rate.
2. Patient Analytics
Analyzes patient demographics, gender distribution, insurance information, and patient activity.
3. Hospital Operations
Monitors admissions, discharges, length of stay, department performance, and admission trends.
4. Doctor & Department Performance
Evaluates doctor activity, appointment volume, salary distribution, and department contributions.

5. Financial Analysis
Provides insights into hospital billing, gross charges, treatment revenue, and financial performance.
6. Quality & Management Insights
Focuses on operational quality, patient waiting time, appointment cancellation, and management KPIs.
